inference. This is an expert-token routing budget, not a parameter-count reduction

references. No new candidate may be promoted at a higher capacity.

Complete this phase with action-conditioned, multi-step outcome learning; durable

persistence error; shuffled-action error at least 125% of correctly conditioned

Information-seeking must improve held-out discoveries by at least 0.1 over random
and no-action policies with equal action budgets. Report all failures and compare
both action counts and elapsed time. Memory must survive restart, isolate entity
and environment identities, honor evaluation partitions, and deduplicate events.

local offline Ubuntu 22.04 checks remain required here. Collision, pain, 3D and
automatic capacity growth remain later milestones. Do not equate prediction loss,
novelty counters or scripted exploration with general curiosity or reasoning.
